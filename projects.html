somars – team captain / vision lead

they fly now????

the suas competition (student unmanned aerial systems) is a globally held event where university teams build autonomous drone systems capable of completing several different task. for 2026 (which is our first time ucsc is competiting), this years theme is search and rescue meaning that our main goals is mapping out areas hit by storms for first responders to assess, locating survivors and dropping supply packages at designated locations.

my first role in the team is serving as the team captain. since this was the first time in the universities history where they had a suas team, i had to reorganize the team and i ended up drawing alot of inspiration from more established teams like uc irvine and berkeley. i created new subteams for various challenges and appointed leads for each one to run it. my secondary role is being the vision lead where we are in charge of the computer vision and mapping algorithms.

mate – integration lead

there's not supposed to be water in the enclosure

(quick clarification, mate is the name of the robotics team at ucsc (aka mate team or sometimes the submarine team) AND the name of the competition (mate rov)) ok thanks bye

the mate rov competition is a globally held tournament where university teams build underwater rovs (remotely operated vehicles) to compete simulated scenarios that real world rov teams face in the ocean. in 2025, the comp was held in Alpena, Michigan and the theme was shipwreck surveillance and exploration. for 2025, this time held in St Johns, Canada, the new theme is icesheet / sub-freezing exploration. some challenges include fish counting, water sample testing, replacing fuses and 3d scanning coral reefs.

the most important component of an rov is the enclosure. this is where we house all of our electronics, esc, sensors and microcontrollers. this enclosure is powered via a tether that runs from the rov to the shore and carries both power and ethernet so our computer can control the rov. my role in the mate team is to design this enclosure and ensure power is properly distributed to all components.
